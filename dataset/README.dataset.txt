## Ways to Use UA-DETRAC-DATASET-10K Model

1. **Intelligent Traffic Flow Analysis:** Integrate the model into city-wide camera networks to monitor vehicle density in real-time, allowing traffic management centers to optimize signal timings based on the volume of cars and buses.
2. **Heavy Vehicle Monitoring and Routing:** Deploy the model on highway gantries to identify trucks and large vans, helping authorities enforce lane restrictions or redirect heavy traffic away from residential areas or low-clearance bridges.
3. **Smart Toll and Access Control:** Enhance automated tolling systems by using vehicle classification to apply differential pricing for cars versus commercial trucks, ensuring accurate billing without manual intervention.
4. **Autonomous Infrastructure Planning:** Use the detection data to generate long-term analytics on road usage by vehicle type, providing urban planners with objective evidence to justify the expansion of bus lanes or freight corridors.
5. **Parking Management Systems:** Implement the model in large-scale parking facilities to track the occupancy of oversized vehicle spots (for vans and buses) versus standard car spots, improving overall lot efficiency.
